About TSPetCare

Our Story

I started my journey in pet care back in 2023 on Rover, where I discovered just how much I loved caring for animals and being someone pet parents could truly rely on. Not long after, life took me in a different direction and I stepped away for a couple of years, but the passion never left.

Our Mission

In 2025, I came back to pet care with a fresh perspective and a clear vision, and that’s when everything really clicked. What once felt like a small side venture finally became the dream I had always imagined, building meaningful connections, creating a safe and comforting experience for every pet, and giving owners complete peace of mind while they’re away. I treat every pet as if they were my own, paying close attention to their routines, personalities, and unique needs. Whether it’s a walk, a drop-in visit, or just some extra love and attention, my goal is always the same: to make sure your pet feels safe, happy, and cared for every step of the way. This isn’t just a service for me, it’s something I genuinely care about, and I’m so grateful to do what I love every day.

Our Team

I’m a certified pet sitter and dog walker, fully insured, and trained in pet CPR and first aid. Your pet’s safety, comfort, and routine are always my top priorities, and I take pride in providing reliable, attentive care you can feel confident about.
